The frontier, as it stands this week.

Capability at the top of the market has converged. Distribution has not. ChatGPT still takes more than half of standalone chatbot web traffic, Gemini is the only other consumer-scale assistant, and Claude now leads on coding and enterprise revenue even with a much smaller public audience. This page compares the latest flagship models, who uses them, and how much capital sits behind each lab.

Who people actually open

Worldwide web-visit share among the seven largest standalone generative assistants, May 2026. Combined traffic that month: 10.4 billion visits. This is the cleanest public ranking of destination chat products — it undercounts apps, APIs, and assistants buried inside Search, Windows, or Instagram.

Source: Similarweb via Momentic, May 2026 complete month. Shares are of this seven-assistant set, not of all AI usage on Earth.

53.9% ChatGPT leads

Two companies hold 82% of measured visits. ChatGPT is still larger than the other six combined, but its share has slid from about 79% a year earlier while its visit count stayed roughly flat. The market grew around it.

Gemini is the only real consumer challenger. 27.9% worldwide, up from the mid-single digits in early 2025, helped by Android, Search, and Workspace. Google said the Gemini app itself crossed 1 billion monthly users this month.

Claude is the growth story, not the volume story. 9.2% of web visits, up about 855% year over year, with the lowest bounce rate among the big three. Anthropic’s money is in the API, not the consumer homepage.

Not on this pie: Meta AI’s 1.2 billion in-app users, Chinese destination apps such as Doubao and Qwen Chat, and almost all Copilot usage inside Microsoft 365.

If you want one assistant

GPT-5.6 in ChatGPT

Still the product with habit, plugins, and a billion people already there. Capability is no longer a unique advantage; the account is.

If you write software

Claude Opus 5

Coding and agent boards flipped to Anthropic in August. Fable 5 if the job is long and expensive. Sonnet if you are paying the bill.

If tokens are the product

DeepSeek V4-Flash

Frontier-adjacent quality at $0.14 / $0.28. The reason Western labs cut Luna and Terra at the end of July.

If you need it to be free and everywhere

Gemini

A billion app users plus Search and Android. Best science scores among the consumer flagships earlier this summer.

If you live on X

Grok 4.5

Native firehose, fewer refusals, and a parent with rocket-scale capex. Not the intelligence leader; the context leader for that network.

The money plot

Anthropic $47B vs OpenAI ~$25B

Consumer fame and API fortune have decoupled. Claude’s user base is a fraction of ChatGPT’s; its run-rate is not.
